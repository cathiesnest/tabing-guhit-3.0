🔎 Find Your Niche

Not sure where your skills fit online? Answer five simple questions and get a starting direction based on your interests and strengths.

1. What do you enjoy doing most?
2. What type of work feels natural to you?
3. What would you rather avoid?
4. What are you most comfortable learning?
5. What matters most to you in work?
